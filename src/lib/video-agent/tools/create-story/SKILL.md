# Funny/Cute Story

Write story before hook, dialogue, shots, or production.

Find character, desire, conflict, causality, and change. Every event follows because or but; never merely “and then.” Establish anything before it becomes a solution.

Build one comic mechanism:

- expectation: viewer predicts what follows;
- subversion: outcome reframes that setup instead of ignoring it;
- timing: prediction forms before reveal;
- benign violation: logic, norms, or dignity bend without harm.

End on the payoff. Put the punchline in the final visible beat, or follow it only with a brief comic button that extends it. Never use cooldown as ending.

Preserve creator request as story subject. Keep result short enough for 4–15 seconds.
Make creator-requested outcome and its cause happen visibly in story events; explanation cannot substitute for causality.

When creator request centers clothing, product, or prop, treat it as featured object rather than scenery. Invent one coherent visual concept that is distinctive enough to recognize on its own, desirable throughout the joke, and essential to causality. Lock that concept from first appearance through ending. For pet canon, featured object must feel unmistakably cute and pet-scaled, never basic. Choose design from this story instead of applying a canned style checklist.

When canon identifies a pet, never invent cast. Use canonical pet plus only characters explicitly requested by creator. At most one pet owner may otherwise participate, and only when creator request or causal action needs them. Represent owner explicitly as an off-camera hand or forearm with neutral medium skin tone and no identifiable racial cues; never invent or show owner face, body, identity, or appearance. Owner voice may remain off-camera. Never invent family, children, crowds, strangers, audiences, extra animals, or other characters.

Return one concise natural-language story, one sentence explaining causal chain and joke, and when applicable one `Featured object lock:` sentence. No hook, dialogue, narration, camera, shots, or JSON.
