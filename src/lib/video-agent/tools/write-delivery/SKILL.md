# Delivery Writing

Privately test narrator-only, character-only, and hybrid readings. Choose strongest execution, not favorite mode. Speech adds character, subtext, irony, compression, or timing; it never describes visible action.

Open with locked hook. If hook previews later moment, return cleanly to setup and fulfill promise. Add no plot, prop, obstacle, solution, or ending.

Design for at most 15 seconds. Total spoken dialogue and narration must be 32 words or fewer. Preserve each speaker’s canon voice identity; only emotion and pace may change. Narrator and character sound distinct.
End timeline on story payoff or its brief comic button. Final window must contain visible action or change; “hold for the laugh” is cooldown.

Return chosen mode, voice lock, timing, silence, sound, and music in natural Markdown. Put every spoken line on its own line as `SPEECH: SPEAKER | exact words | performance`. No other line may contain spoken words. No JSON.
